# Geospatial analysis

Check latitude/longitude order, units, coordinate reference system, and missing or invalid coordinates before calculations. Use a suitable method for distance or spatial joins, and compare sample results with known points or bounds. Keep zone definitions and the pipeline's existing geography contracts consistent. Do not introduce H3 or a new geometry representation unless the task requires it.
